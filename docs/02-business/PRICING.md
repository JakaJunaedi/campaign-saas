# Pricing Strategy (Proposed) — Campaign SaaS

## 1. Document Control
- **Title**: Pricing Strategy & Tier Specification
- **Purpose**: Usulan struktur paket harga untuk validasi model bisnis pasca-pilot MVP.
- **Status**: PROPOSED
- **Scope**: Commercial Packaging

---

## 2. Proposed Subscription Tiers

| Paket | Target Agensi | Fitur & Kapasitas Utama | Indikasi Harga (IDR / Bulan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter Agency** | Tim 1-5 staf, baru merintis agensi KOL | Maks 5 Active Campaigns, 3 Team Seats, 50 Creators CRM, 20GB Storage | Rp 750.000 / bln |
| **Growth Agency** | Tim 6-20 staf, agensi berkembang aktif | Maks 25 Active Campaigns, 10 Team Seats, 500 Creators CRM, 100GB Storage, Custom PDF Branding | Rp 2.500.000 / bln |
| **Scale / Enterprise** | Agensi besar dengan ratusan campaign | Unlimited Campaigns, Unlimited Seats, Unlimited Creators, 500GB+ Storage, Priority Support | Rp 6.000.000+ / bln |
